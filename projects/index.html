Periodic Table of Elements

Live Application
vivekgautham.github.io/periodictable/

An interactive, high-resolution atomic science exploration platform of the IUPAC Periodic Table of Elements. Delivers comprehensive physicochemical properties, electron shell configurations, isotopic decay profiles, and thermodynamic phase boundaries across all 118 chemical elements.

Key Capabilities & Features
  • All 118 Elements Profiled: Standard atomic weights, quantum orbital electron configurations ($s, p, d, f$ blocks), oxidation states, atomic radii, and ionization energies.
  • Thermodynamic & Physical Parameters: Accurate melting and boiling points, phases of matter at standard temperature and pressure (STP), density ($\text{g/cm}^3$), and Pauling electronegativity scales.
  • Isotopic Stability & Radioactive Decay: Complete isotopic catalog charting stable nuclides, radioisotopes, natural half-lives, and alpha ($\alpha$), beta ($\beta$), and gamma decay pathways.
  • Interactive Chemical Series: Instant color-coded filtering across alkali metals, alkaline earths, transition metals, post-transition metals, metalloids, reactive nonmetals, noble gases, lanthanides, and actinides.
  • In-Place Element Drawer & Visuals: Interactive element drawer featuring 2D/3D Bohr atomic models, spectral emission lines, CPK molecular coloring, discovery history, and etymology.
